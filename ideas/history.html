Harbord history

From Duke Kahanamoku’s 1914 wave to today, plus a place for locals to share their memories.

Verified facts only
1914

Duke rides Freshwater

On 24 December 1914 Hawaiian legend Duke Kahanamoku surfed at Freshwater, the beach the Harbord now calls the birthplace of Australian surfing.

1928

The Harbord Hilton

The hotel is built in 1928, when the suburb was still called Harbord. Locals soon nickname the buttercup-yellow pub the “Harbord Hilton”.

42 yrs

John Thorpe & Trish King

For 42 years John Thorpe and Trish King run the Harbord.

2020

New custodians

Freshwater locals Glenn Piper and Lachlan Cottee buy the Harbord in 2020.

2021

Doors reopen

After a major refurbishment the Harbord reopens on 3 January 2021 as the Freshwater Beach House.

2025

Bombies arrives

The 70s-inspired rooftop bar and band room, Bombies, opens upstairs.
