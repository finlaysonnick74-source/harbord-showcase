Orders and payments land in your POS

Your POS stays the source of truth. Orders arrive as normal POS tickets through the POS maker’s official API, so they print and report exactly like a bar order. Payments settle through your existing provider. The Harbord already takes QR orders at the table; this layer can run alongside it or replace it.

Guest phone

Scans table card. Menu, modifiers, notes, Apple Pay, Google Pay or card.

→

BuildIT order layer

A small Cloudflare Worker. Checks sold-out items, prices and table, then hands the order on. Keeps no card data.

→

Your POS

Order posted as a ticket via the official API, against the right table and printer or kitchen screen.

→

Kitchen & bar

Ticket prints or shows on screen. Status flows back to the guest’s phone.

Works with your existing POS

We connect to the system you already run. Common Australian hospitality options we can build against:

LightspeedSwiftPOSH&LBepoz

Payments

Card details go straight from the guest’s phone to the payment provider’s secure checkout. Funds settle to the venue as they do today, and the payment is matched to the POS order.

Same menu everywhere

Prices, items and sold-out status are read from the POS or one shared sheet, so the QR menu, the website and the bar never disagree.

Built for a busy Sunday

Real Harbord menu with size choices, add-ons like Parmi it or Surf your Turf, and gluten-free, dairy-free and vegan options.

Sold out means sold out

Items marked sold out can’t be added, and a cart with a newly sold-out item asks the guest to swap before paying.

Fast on beach signal

Under 100 KB of code, no app to download, works on any phone that can scan a QR code.
