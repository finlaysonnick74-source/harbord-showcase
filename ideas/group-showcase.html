A suggested rollout

Start small at the Harbord, prove it on a busy weekend, then roll across the group.

Step 1

Harbord menu

QR menu, sold-out switch and table cards on the real menu, alongside current ordering.

Step 2

Order & pay

Connect to the POS and payment provider, pilot in one area, then the whole venue.

Step 3

Every venue

Re-skin per venue from one engine; add specials, What’s On and AI search profiles.

Step 4

Story & growth

Functions microsites, the history wall and the 3D tour for pre-opening marketing.
